Photo to Scan

Turn a photograph of a paper document into a straight, readable scan on your device.

🔒 Your files never leave your machine. Photo to Scan works with your document photo right on this device. Nothing is uploaded for processing.

Select a document photo

Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP image file up to 15 MiB.

1. Adjust corners

Use Detect Edges for a starting point, then drag the four corner handles or use Arrow keys (Shift for larger steps) to fine-tune document boundaries.

2. Scanned document

Perspective correction straightens the selected quadrilateral into a flat scan.

ℹ️ Output Notice: Exported JPEG and PDF files are image-based raster downloads and do not contain searchable text.

Straighten document photographs locally

Photo to Scan can find a high-contrast document boundary on your device to set a starting point. Review and adjust the four corners as needed, then select color, grayscale or high-contrast processing and export a clean JPEG or PDF.

Private by design

Your files never leave your machine. Your document photo stays strictly on this device while processing happens inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to a remote server.
