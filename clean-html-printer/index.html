Up to 2 MB. We use a clean reading layout, not an exact copy of the website. Scripts and original styles are removed. Embedded PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP images are kept; remote images and companion files are not loaded.

Real removal. Not a white overlay.

Images, paragraphs, tables and whole sections can be removed from the document. The remaining content flows together. Undo or reset at any time; your original file stays unchanged.

A simpler page, by design

Complex web layouts become readable blocks. Links are kept as text, forms and embedded media are omitted, and online images are not fetched. This helps protect your privacy and avoids empty spaces left by fixed website layouts.
