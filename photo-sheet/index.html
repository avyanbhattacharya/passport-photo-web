How it works

Import several photos, pick a grid layout (1, 2, 4, 6, or 9 per page), choose Letter or A4 size, adjust margins and gaps, and export a print-ready single-page PDF.

Fit vs. Crop framing

Choose Fit to preserve each photo's original aspect ratio without losing any part of the image, or choose Crop to fill each grid cell completely.

Print scaling instructions

When printing the downloaded PDF, select Actual Size or 100% scale in your PDF reader or browser print dialog so the sheet prints with exact physical dimensions.

Private by design

Your photos stay on this device while the sheet is made. Helper scripts may load in your browser, but your photos are not sent away for processing.
