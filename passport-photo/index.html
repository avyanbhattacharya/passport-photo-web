Common passport photo questions

Can I make a passport photo from an iPhone?

Yes. Start the camera in Safari, follow the framing guidance, take the photo and adjust it in the browser. You can also choose an existing image from the phone.

Can this make the background white?

Yes. The optional white-background tool uses browser-side person segmentation and composites the existing foreground pixels over white. Inspect hair, ears, shoulders and clothing edges carefully.

Does this tool upload my passport photo?

The project does not include a photo-upload API or server-side image-processing service. The working photo is processed locally in the browser. Optional machine-learning code and model files are downloaded when those features are used.

Does the automated check guarantee passport acceptance?

No. Passport and visa rules vary, and the issuing authority makes the acceptance decision.

Privacy first: processing stays in your browser

Your passport photo is sensitive. Optional ML models are downloaded when needed, but your working photo is processed by the browser on your device.
