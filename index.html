Privacy first: processing stays in your browser

Your passport photo is sensitive. This app is designed so the working photo does not need to be uploaded to this project's server. Camera capture, cropping, positioning, brightness and contrast adjustments, automated checks, background processing, JPEG creation and 4×6 print-sheet generation happen locally in your browser. The optional ML models are downloaded when needed, but your working photo is processed by the browser on your device. Reloading or closing the page clears the working image.

Important

Passport and visa photo rules vary by country and document type. Some authorities may restrict digital background replacement or retouching. Always verify the current requirements from the issuing authority. This tool does not guarantee acceptance.
