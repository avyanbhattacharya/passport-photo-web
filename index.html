Camera tips
  • Follow the live guidance for centering, distance and head tilt.
  • Use a plain, light-colored wall and even front lighting.
  • Look directly at the camera with a neutral expression.
  • Keep the entire head, both sides of the face and shoulders visible.
  • Avoid glasses, harsh shadows and filters.

Privacy first: processing stays in your browser

Your passport photo is sensitive. This app is designed so the working photo does not need to be uploaded to this project's server. Camera capture, live framing feedback, automatic positioning, cropping, brightness and contrast adjustments, automated checks, blur/shadow scoring, background processing, JPEG creation and 4×6 print-sheet generation happen locally in your browser. The optional ML models are downloaded when needed, but your working photo is processed by the browser on your device.

Offline/PWA: the core app can be cached by your browser and installed to your home screen. Optional ML features may still require their model files to have been downloaded previously. On iPhone, use Safari’s Share menu → Add to Home Screen.

Important

Passport and visa photo rules vary by country and document type. Some authorities may restrict digital background replacement or retouching. Always verify the current requirements from the issuing authority. This tool does not guarantee acceptance.
