Free automatic Japa counter

Choose a pace and let the counter advance automatically while you practice. It tracks your total naam, completed rounds of 108 and elapsed time. Prefer manual counting? Use the Tap Japa Counter.

Private and local

Your count is stored in local browser storage on this device. The tool does not need to send your Japa session to an application server.

108-count round tracking

Every 108 counts completes one round. If an iPhone suspends the page while the screen is locked, the counter uses elapsed time to catch up when you return.
